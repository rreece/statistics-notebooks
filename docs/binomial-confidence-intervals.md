## Wald

The Wald confidence interval (CI) at $(1-\alpha)$ confidence level (CL) is


```math
\hat{\mu} \pm z_{\alpha/2} \sigma_{\hat{\mu}}, \qquad \sigma_{\hat{\mu}} = \sqrt{\frac{\hat{\mu} (1 - \hat{\mu})}{n}}

```
